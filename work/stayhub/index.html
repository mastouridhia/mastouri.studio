StayHub

  • Mobile App
  • UX | UI
  • React Native
  • Maps & Geo
  • Authentication

StayHub is a mobile app for discovering and booking unique places to stay. Built with React Native, Expo and TypeScript, it combines a listings feed and a live map in one explore screen, a step-by-step search flow, rich property pages and social sign-in - a full booking-app experience, designed and developed end to end.

Role
Mobile App Design & Development
Stack
React Native · Expo · TypeScript
Year
2025
Team
Solo

(My Role)

  • Designed and built every screen, from sign-in to the reservation footer.
  • Built the explore experience: categories, a draggable listings sheet and a clustered price map.
  • Built the search flow as a blurred modal of three collapsing steps: where, when and who.
  • Set up authentication with Clerk - Google, Apple and Facebook sign-in, with tokens kept in secure storage.

Approach

01

Explore First

The app opens straight onto stays. Search and categories sit in one sticky header, so browsing never takes more than a tap.

02

Map & List as One

Instead of two separate views, the listings live in a bottom sheet over the map. Pull it down to see the map, swipe it up for the list.

03

Search as a Flow

Destination, dates and guests are three cards that open one at a time. Each finished step collapses to a one-line summary.

04

Motion & Feel

Reanimated transitions, a scroll-driven parallax header and light haptic feedback make the app feel native rather than web-like.

(Part 02)

Under the Hood

How the key screens were built: the problem each one solves and the tools behind it.

(System 01 · Map Exploration)

Hundreds of homes, one readable map

Showing every listing as a pin turns a city into noise. Nearby listings merge into white count bubbles that split apart as you zoom in, until each one becomes a price marker. Tapping a price opens that listing.

Clustering
react-native-map-clustering with a custom cluster renderer
Location
“Locate me” centres the map on the user via expo-location
Data
GeoJSON listings feed rendered as markers

(System 02 · Search Flow)

Where, when, who - one step at a time

Search opens as a transparent modal over a blurred explore screen, so you never lose your place. Only one card is open at a time; the others collapse to a one-line summary you can tap to go back.

Where
Destination search with visual region picks
When
Inline calendar date picker
Who
Adults, children, infants and pets counters

(System 03 · Listing Page)

A property page that moves with you

The photo header scales and slides with the scroll position, and a solid header fades in once the image is gone. Price and the reserve button stay pinned to the bottom, and the native share sheet is one tap away.

Parallax
Reanimated scroll offset, interpolated
Actions
Share, save and reserve
Routing
Dynamic route per listing with Expo Router

(System 04 · Authentication)

Sign in without friction

If you're not signed in when the app starts, the login modal opens on its own. Google, Apple and Facebook run through Clerk OAuth, and session tokens are cached in the device's secure store.

Provider
Clerk (@clerk/clerk-expo)
Storage
expo-secure-store token cache
Profile
Editable name and avatar from the photo library
