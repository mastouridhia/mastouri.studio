Web Developer Intern · January 2024

Live at atej.tn

ATEJ (Association Tunisienne de l'Enfance et de la Jeunesse) is a Tunisian youth association that runs projects with children, adolescents and young people. In January 2024, for my first internship, I built their website with WordPress. It was the first site I shipped for a real organization, and it is still online.

(My Role)

  • Built the association's website in WordPress and put it live at atej.tn.
  • Organized their content into five pages: home, about, team, latest news and contact.
  • Worked with Arabic, right-to-left content on desktop and on phones.

(The Brief)

ATEJ needed one place online that explains who they are, shows their projects and partners, and lets people follow their news and reach them. Most visitors are young people on their phones, and the content is in Arabic, written in a friendly Tunisian voice.

(The Result)

A live website at atej.tn that the association uses to present its work. It was my first real client project: the first time I built for someone else's content, audience and language instead of my own.
